## DiskMate v2.1.0 — 首个公开版本

### 中文
1. 按需扫描：打开只到欢迎页，点「开始扫描」才动磁盘，13 步体检全程可取消
2. 每个项目都标注风险等级、删除后果和真实路径，默认只勾低风险
3. 三个页面：清理体检 / 大文件 / 日志；结果页支持搜索、排序、筛选、批量勾选
4. 应用内更新：左下角一键检查、下载、SHA-256 校验、退出后替换并自动重启
5. 10 种界面语言（简体中文默认），切换即时生效并记忆
6. 单文件 exe，免安装，只用系统自带 .NET Framework 4.x

### English
1. Scan on demand: the app opens on a welcome screen; nothing is read until you press "Start scan"; all 13 steps are cancellable
2. Every item shows its risk level, what deletion does and the real path; only low-risk items are pre-selected
3. Three pages: clean check, large files, log; results support search, sorting, filtering and multi-select
4. In-app updates: one click to check, download, verify with SHA-256, then swap and relaunch
5. 10 interface languages (Simplified Chinese by default), switched instantly and remembered
6. Single exe, no installer, uses only the built-in .NET Framework 4.x
